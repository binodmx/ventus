● Ventus Wind Tunnel Designs is a framework for the design specifications for Subsonic, Low-Speed, Open Circuit wind tunnels.
● Follow through the guidelines and you can build your own wind tunnel.
● Your wind tunnel design can be built upon the test section dimensions or the specifications of the fan you use.
● Please note that this framework assumes a square shaped cross section in the test section.
